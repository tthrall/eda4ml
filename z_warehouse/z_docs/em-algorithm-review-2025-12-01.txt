## Appendix A: EM Algorithm — Review

**File**: em-algorithm.qmd | **Lines**: 568 | **Sections**: 8

---

### Core Concept

The Expectation-Maximization algorithm for fitting mixture models, demonstrated through Gaussian mixture modeling of Old Faithful eruption data. Serves as mathematical foundation for LDA topic models.

---

### Key Skills

- Understand latent variable models
- Formulate Gaussian mixture models
- Initialize EM algorithm with reasonable starting values
- Interpret EM iterations and convergence
- Apply `mixtools::mvnormalmixEM()` in R

---

### Mathematical Prerequisites

**Moderate.** Requires: multivariate normal distribution, Mahalanobis distance, likelihood functions, iterative optimization. Comparable to Chapter 9 (LDA).

---

### Strengths

1. **Code is shown!** (line 430): The `mvnormalmixEM()` call is visible with `echo: true`. This is the first appendix/chapter to show substantial R code.
2. **Excellent motivating example**: Old Faithful's two clusters are visually obvious, making the mixture model intuitive
3. **Careful initialization** (lines 136-229): Step-by-step procedure for obtaining starting values—often glossed over in EM treatments
4. **Iteration output visible** (lines 456-458): `verb = TRUE` shows students the convergence progress
5. **Posterior interpretation** (lines 529-556): The temporal pattern of group membership is a nice addition
6. **Clear connection to LDA** (lines 53-57): Explicitly states this prepares for understanding topic models
7. **Good visualizations**: Scatter plots with 50% ellipses make the clusters tangible

---

### Weaknesses

1. **No general EM formulation**: The E-step and M-step are described informally (line 132) but the general algorithm (Q-function, Jensen's inequality) isn't presented
2. **Only Gaussian mixtures**: EM applies broadly, but only this case is shown
3. **No convergence theory**: Why does EM converge? What can go wrong?
4. **Missing connection to Chapter 3**: Clustering chapter uses k-means; EM is "soft" clustering—connection not explicit
5. **Typos** (lines 92-93): "delay (minutes" and "duration (minutes" missing closing parentheses

---

### EDA Connection

**Implicit.** The scatter plot identifying two clusters is EDA, but not framed that way.

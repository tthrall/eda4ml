## Chapter 13: Time Series Spectrum Analysis — Review

**File**: ts-fourier.qmd | **Lines**: 1,509 | **Sections**: 10

---

### Core Concept

Frequency-domain analysis: representing time series as sums of sinusoids, periodogram estimation, spectral density, coherence between series. Complements Chapter 12's time-domain approach.

---

### Key Skills

- Understand Fourier decomposition of time series
- Compute and interpret periodograms
- Estimate spectral density
- Interpret coherence and phase between series
- Connect frequency and time domain representations

---

### Mathematical Prerequisites

**Substantial.** Requires: complex exponentials, Fourier series, integration, matrix-valued functions. Comparable to Chapter 12 in difficulty.

1. **Significant duplication**: The stationarity/ACF framework appears in all three chapters. Consolidate in Chapter 11 and reference forward.
2. **Code consistently hidden**: None of the time series chapters show R code. Students don't see `ts()`, `arima()`, `spec.pgram()`, or any of the astsa/fable/forecast functions.
3. **No exercises in any chapter**: All three chapters lack practice problems.
4. **Chapter 12 length**: At 2,833 lines, it's the second-longest chapter and should probably be split.
5. **Strong mathematical treatment**: All three chapters develop the theory carefully—this is a strength, but needs to be balanced with visible code.
6. **Good use of astsa package**: The textbook tie-in is valuable for students who want to go deeper.

## Chapter 12: Time Series Forecasting — Review

**File**: ts-forecast.qmd | **Lines**: 2,833 | **Sections**: 15+

---

### Core Concept

Time-domain modeling and forecasting: smoothing, differencing, ARMA models, and prediction. Develops AR, MA, and ARIMA models from first principles with emphasis on polynomial operator notation.

---

### Key Skills

- Apply moving averages and exponential smoothing
- Use differencing to remove trends
- Understand AR(p) and MA(q) model structures
- Work with backshift operator notation
- Interpret ACF and PACF for model identification
- Forecast using ARIMA models

---

### Mathematical Prerequisites

**Substantial.** Requires: polynomial algebra, operator notation, power series, complex roots, recursive algorithms. The most mathematically demanding chapter in Part 4.

---

3. **Clear AR(1) worked example** (lines 646-738): Step-by-step derivation of ACF and PACF
4. **Practical cardiovascular mortality example** (lines 555-602): Shows iterative model-building process
5. **Causality and invertibility** (lines 638-644): Explains why root conditions matter
6. **Comprehensive glossary** (lines 2580-2670): Excellent notation reference

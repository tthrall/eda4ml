## Chapter 11: Time Series Data Analysis — Review

**File**: ts-intro.qmd | **Lines**: 1,048 | **Sections**: 12

### Core Concept

Survey of time series data types and contexts: natural science (sunspots, climate, predator-prey), business/economics (earnings, stock indices), and signal processing (fMRI, speech, seismology). Introduces stationarity, autocorrelation, and effective sample size.

---

### Key Skills

- Recognize time series data structures and contexts
- Understand second-order stationarity
- Compute and interpret autocorrelation functions
- Recognize the impact of autocorrelation on variance estimation
- Calculate effective sample size (ESS)

---

### Mathematical Prerequisites

**Moderate.** Requires covariance, correlation, expectation operators, summation notation. The MA(1) variance derivation (lines 780-843) is more demanding but clearly worked through.

2. **Context sections for each example**: "Questions and Objectives" paragraphs (lines 111-114, 150-154, etc.) explain _why_ we analyze each dataset

### Weaknesses

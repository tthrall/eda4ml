## Exercises {#sec-ts-freq-domain-exercises}

1. **Periodogram variability**: Generate 256 observations of white noise and compute the periodogram using `stats::spec.pgram()` with no smoothing (`spans = NULL`). What is the approximate range of periodogram values? Repeat with `spans = c(7,7)`. How does smoothing affect the variability?

2. **AR spectrum identification**: Using `astsa::arma.spec()`, plot the theoretical spectra of AR(1) processes with $\phi = 0.9$, $\phi = 0.5$, and $\phi = -0.5$. How does the AR parameter affect spectrum shape?

3. **Sunspot period**: From the sunspot spectrum, estimate the dominant period. How does this compare to the commonly cited "11-year solar cycle"? What does the width of the spectral peak suggest about period variability?

4. **Temperature seasonality**: For the global land temperature series (`astsa::gtemp_land`), estimate and plot the spectrum. Does it show evidence of annual periodicity? What feature dominates the spectrum, and what does this indicate about the series?

5. **Smoothing comparison**: For the sunspot data, compare spectrum estimates with `spans = c(3,3)`, `spans = c(7,7)`, and `spans = c(15,15)`. How does bandwidth affect the appearance of the dominant peak and the noise level?

6. **Coherence interpretation**: For two economic series (e.g., GDP and unemployment), estimate the coherence spectrum. At what frequencies is coherence highest? What might this indicate about the relationship between the series?

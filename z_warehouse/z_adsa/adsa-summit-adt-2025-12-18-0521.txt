----- begin Tony T bio-paragraph -----

  Dr. Thrall served with the National Security Agency (NSA) from 2015 to 2025, and now continues to work with colleagues to advance data science teaching and learning.  At NSA Dr. Thrall led an effort to update the Data Science Competency Resource Guide released in 2023 by the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI).  Subsequently Dr. Thrall led efforts to develop assessments and course content aligned with the updated standards.  Dr. Thrall has recently completed and online book, "Exploratory Data Analysis for Machine Learning" based on an internal course at NSA.
  
  Prior to joining NSA Dr. Thrall worked in the tech industry in Silicon Valley and in the Insurance and Financial sectors in New York.  He earned his MA in Education from Stanford University and his PhD in Statistics from UC Berkeley.

----- end Tony T bio-paragraph -----

----- begin Tony T interests and Summit themes -----

  As a former hiring manager of data scientists in both the public and private sectors, I have a keen interest in clarifying the the data science skills needed by employers and the corresponding qualifications of applicants.  With the rapid development of data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence, it is in the interests of all stakeholder to improve the efficiency of the skills marketplace.
  
  In addition, my recent book, "Exploratory Data Analysis for Machine Learning", advocates for hybrid skills and workflows that use both classical and AI-based tools.

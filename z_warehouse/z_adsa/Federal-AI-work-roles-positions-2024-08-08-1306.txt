As I understand it, you are currently mapping work roles to the data lifecycle and Master program guidance for DS. It may be beneficial to share a bit of what NSF found about Federal AI roles and positions back in March. Background research was done by MITRE - I believe Jessica and Karri attended the last STIDS meeting.

      

Attached is our original list of work roles and positions. Jessica and MITRE team worked with workforce SMEs to recognize the difference between a position (title describing a single person’s job at an organization) and a work role (a grouping of tasks/work that can allow for more efficient education, training, hiring, and conducting of the work etc.). From what we gathered when you look at job postings, often you are reading position titles. However, someone filling that position may “wear many hats”, conducting work within multiple work roles. According to OPM classification, what is posted under a single position description, may include up to 3 work roles listed indicating responsibilities according to the taxonomy of those roles. In the attached sheet, work roles are colored orange as where positions are purple. Of the work roles we started with in this sheet, the following merges across sources were identified:

 

Data Analysis in the NICE Framework has a cyber-specific definition, whereas definitions for the Data Analyst role in the DCWF and GSA’s List of AI-Ready Work Roles are generalizable. Therefore, the DCWF and GSA’s Data Analyst roles should be merged but Data Analysis in the NICE Framework should remain separate. 
Data Scientist in the DCWF and GSA’s List of AI-Ready Work Roles, as well as Interdisciplinary Data Scientist in OPM’s Draft Interpretive Guidance AI Work Roles, share common definitions and should therefore be merged. 
Privacy Compliance in the NICE Framework and Privacy Compliance Manager in the DCWF share common definitions and should therefore be merged. 
Program Management in the NICE Framework and Program Manager in the DCWF share common definitions and should therefore be merged. 
Secure Software Development in the NICE Framework and Software Developer in the DCWF share common definitions and should therefore be merged. 
Target Network Analysis in the NICE Framework and Target Network Analyst in the DCWF share common definitions and should therefore be merged. 
Threat Analysis in the NICE Framework and Warning Analyst in the DCWF share common definitions and should therefore be merged.
 

I hope it can help with your work and looking forward for your comments at the next meeting. Thank you - VP

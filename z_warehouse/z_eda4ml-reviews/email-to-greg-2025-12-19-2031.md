**Setup:**

Clone these three repos to your local machine:

Then in R:
```
remotes::install_github("tthrall/eda4mldata")
```

To render the book:
```

```

Open `_book/index.html` in your browser. The search function (magnifying glass icon) works across all chapters, which makes navigation easier.
